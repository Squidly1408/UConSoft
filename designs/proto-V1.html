About UoN Social

Purpose‑built for three outcomes: (1) a living resume of student projects, (2) a marketing window into UoN’s learner‑made innovation, and (3) a browsable WIL portfolio directory for companies.

  • Fast mobile UX: large tap targets, grid cards, sticky actions.
  • Accessible: skip link, ARIA labels, keyboard focus styles.
  • Privacy‑ready: simple local data model; plug any backend later.
